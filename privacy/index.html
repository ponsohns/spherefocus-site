This notice covers the Sphere waitlist website and support email while the macOS app is not yet publicly available.

Effective 2026-08-06

1. Joining the waitlist

The waitlist asks you to provide one thing: your email address. A static public list identifier routes that address to Sphere's waitlist; it does not describe you or track where on the page you signed up.

Sphere uses the address to send product updates and notify you when the app is ready. You can unsubscribe through any list email or ask Sphere support to remove your address.

2. Email provider

Sphere uses Loops, operated by Astrodon Corporation, to receive waitlist submissions, maintain the list, and send Sphere product and launch emails. Loops processes the submitted address and service information needed to operate and protect its form and email service.

Read Loops' privacy policy for its own practices.

3. Website delivery

This static site is hosted through GitHub Pages. Hosting and form services may process ordinary request information such as an IP address, requested path, time, browser information, and security or abuse-prevention events to deliver and protect their services.

Sphere does not add advertising pixels, behavioral analytics, session replay, or tracking cookies to this prelaunch site.

4. Contacting support

If you email support@spherefocus.app, you voluntarily provide your email address, message, and anything you attach. Sphere uses that information to answer you and keeps only what is reasonably needed for support, security, disputes, and legal obligations.

Do not send passwords, payment-card details, identity documents, browsing details, or other secrets in an initial message.

5. Your choices

You may ask about, correct, or request deletion of personal information handled directly by Sphere by emailing support. Some records may need to be kept where required for security, disputes, or applicable law.
