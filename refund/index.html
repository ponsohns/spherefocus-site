Refunds

The policy is decided.
Sales are not open.

Annual: full refund within 7 days of your first charge. Monthly: no refund window. Your legal rights always apply.

Effective 2026-08-06

Annual

A first annual purchase may be refunded in full within 7 days of its initial charge. Annual renewals are excluded from this voluntary window.

Monthly

Monthly plans have no voluntary refund window. Cancelling stops a future renewal; it does not itself refund a completed charge.

Overrides and requests

A refund is still provided where required by applicable law, Polar requirements, non-delivery, duplicate charges, incorrect charges. Statutory rights remain unaffected.

When sales open, refund requests will begin through support@spherefocus.app. Purchases are not currently available.
