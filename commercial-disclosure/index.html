Price and recurring billing

The announced plans are $14.99 per month and $119.99 per year. There is no free trial. When sales open, the selected plan is charged at purchase and renews at its stated interval until cancelled. Applicable taxes and the final total will be shown before payment.

Payment and merchant of record

When checkout opens, Polar will act as merchant of record and reseller for the payment transaction. Polar will administer payment, tax, receipts, subscription billing, and transaction records under its own terms and notices. Polar is not presented as Sphere's software supplier or as the controller for information handled directly by Sphere.

Delivery, cancellation, and refunds

Digital access is intended after successful payment and verified activation. Cancellation stops a future renewal. Annual: full refund within 7 days of your first charge. Monthly: no refund window. Your legal rights always apply. See the refund policy for details. Mandatory legal rights always apply.

Purchases and downloads are not open during prelaunch.
